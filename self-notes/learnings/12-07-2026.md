### Main Learnings

#### 1. Pressure should come from the work, not the expectation
source: [[1-stop-pressuring-yourself]]
- Do not accept artificial pressure from outside expectations.
- Pressure is often fake or socially constructed.
- Let the pressure come from doing the work well, not from chasing appearances or status.

#### 2. Audacity matters
source: [[1-executive-coach-top-0-01-percent-dr-julie-gurner]]
- Take bigger swings.
- Use a "what if this goes right?" frame.
- Approach things with more courage and self-belief.
- Reinforcing beliefs:
  - I can do anything.
  - I can learn anything.

#### 3. Good businesses may live outside the obvious categories
source: [[1-invisible-companies]]
- Profitable businesses are not limited to the standard buckets like media or software.
- There are likely strong businesses in less glamorous or less crowded spaces.
- Look beyond the fashionable categories when evaluating opportunities.

source:[[1-how-to-understand-things]]
