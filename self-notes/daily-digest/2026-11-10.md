# Morning Digest - 2026-11-10

## Main Thought
The Savannah Bananas story shows that a company can compete by redefining what customers are buying. Jesse Cole turned a struggling baseball team into an entertainment experience built around fans, not simply the game. That shift made unusual service and memorable details part of the product rather than decoration.

## For You As A Founder
Ask what experience customers actually value, then design the whole offer around it. A product may be technically sound yet forgettable; one coherent, distinctive promise can make it easier to choose and recommend. Measure the moments that deliver that promise, not just the output you produce.

## For You As A Young Man
Look for ways to make your work genuinely useful and memorable to others. Do not chase attention for its own sake; notice what people value, then bring care and originality to that experience.
