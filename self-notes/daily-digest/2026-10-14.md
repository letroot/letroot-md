# Morning Digest - 2026-10-14

## Main Thought
Your day’s output behaves more like average speed on a trip than a collection of heroic sprints. Long stretches of email, meetings, switching, and drift can pull down the whole day; a short burst of intense focus rarely makes up for them. A bigger gain often comes from removing the recurring drag.

## For You As A Founder
Track a typical week closely enough to identify your three biggest low-value drains. Then remove or contain one: batch messages, shorten a recurring meeting, or protect your first high-energy block for customer work, decisions, or shipping. Check whether the important work moved, not just whether the calendar was full.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not use busyness as proof that you are progressing. Notice where your attention goes, reduce the noise you can control, and give the important task a protected place in your day. Discipline is partly the design of your environment.
