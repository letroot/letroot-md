# Morning Digest - 2026-10-03

## Main Thought
Products rarely lose focus through one dramatic decision. The core job gets crowded out by a stream of small requests that seem easy to add. Each new option must coexist with everything already built, so complexity rises while the original failure remains. Protect the outcome customers came for before expanding the menu.

## For You As A Founder
Name the product's most important promise and make its reliability visible. Before approving a feature, ask which customer outcome it improves, what it will cost to maintain alongside existing behavior, and what committed work it will displace. Keep the highest-impact unfinished item truly first; don't let a parade of easy tickets push it down the queue.

## For You As A Young Man
Learn to say no to attractive distractions, including your own clever ideas. Responsibility means keeping promises, noticing when teammates are carrying hidden strain, and fixing the underlying problem instead of praising another heroic crunch.
