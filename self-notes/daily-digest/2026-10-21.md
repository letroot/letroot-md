# Morning Digest - 2026-10-21

## Main Thought
A strong emotion can be real without being a reliable instruction. Dr. Julie Gurner’s “bad 10 minutes” lesson is that a brief burst of anger or fear can produce consequences that outlast the feeling. The discipline is not to deny the emotion; it is to create enough space to choose what to do with it.

## For You As A Founder
When a deal collapses, a bug lands, or someone frustrates you, put a short delay between the reaction and any irreversible action: sending the message, changing the plan, firing someone, or making a promise. Write down the decision you want to make, revisit it when the spike has passed, then act on the facts and the outcome you want.

## For You As A Young Man
Self-command is not pretending you feel nothing. Notice the heat, name what triggered it, and give yourself time before acting. A pause is useful when it protects your values; afterward, still do the hard conversation or necessary work rather than using the pause to avoid it.
