# Morning Digest - 2026-08-05

## Main Thought
The value of a product is not just what it does; it is the outcome customers believe it enables. Saving money positions you as a cost to negotiate down. Creating growth positions you as an investment worth increasing. The underlying mechanics may be identical, but the buyer's incentives, urgency, and budget are not.

## For You As A Founder
Audit your homepage, demos, and sales conversations for language that leads with efficiency, time saved, or reduced spend. Those benefits matter, but they should usually support the larger outcome: more revenue, more capacity, faster growth, lower risk, or a strategic advantage. Be precise and honest about the causal link. Good positioning is not inflated language; it is choosing the business result your customer actually values most.

## For You As A Young Man
Learn to explain your work in terms of the good it creates, not only the effort it takes. This is not self-promotion for its own sake. It is respect for the other person's attention: show them the result that matters, then let your discipline make the claim credible.
