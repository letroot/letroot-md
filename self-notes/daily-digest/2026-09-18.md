# Morning Digest - 2026-09-18

## Main Thought
You do not need a perfect forecast to make progress; you need to avoid bets that can remove you from the game. Favor experiments with limited downside and meaningful upside. A small failed test can teach you; a large irreversible commitment can make one wrong assumption fatal.

## For You As A Founder
Run a fragility test on one live part of the business: what happens if sales, churn, ad costs, or a key supplier move 20% against you? Reduce the risk that can break the company, then create a small, time-boxed experiment you can double down on or kill within a week. Preserve exits before pursuing scale.

## For You As A Young Man
Build a life that can absorb setbacks: keep your obligations sensible, protect health and character, and take deliberate risks where a miss teaches more than it damages. Courage is not betting your future on every opportunity; it is staying able to take the next good bet.
