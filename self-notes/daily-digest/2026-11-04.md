# Morning Digest - 2026-11-04

## Main Thought
Mercado Libre pairs broad team autonomy with a clear shared direction. Teams are trusted to decide how to reach high-level objectives, while design reviews and firm expectations around quality keep freedom from turning into drift. Leverage comes from making good decisions close to the work, not from routing every choice through a central manager.

## For You As A Founder
Give a teammate or yourself a clear outcome and the boundaries that matter, then leave room to choose the implementation. Review the result against the goal and the quality bar, not whether the path matched your first idea. This keeps ownership close to the problem while preserving alignment.

## For You As A Young Man
Independence is not the absence of standards. Learn to take responsibility for an outcome, make your reasoning visible, and accept honest review. That combination earns more trust and freedom than waiting to be told each next step.
