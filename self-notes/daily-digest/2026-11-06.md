# Morning Digest - 2026-11-06

## Main Thought
Making success the condition for feeling okay creates a moving target: a small win feels insufficient, and a setback can start to feel like a verdict on you. Dr. K’s “dead center” idea is to care about the outcome without letting fear of losing or craving to win take over your attention. Bring it back to the action available now.

## For You As A Founder
Track outcomes to learn, but pair them with controllable practice. A weak activation rate is a signal to inspect onboarding; it is not proof you are a bad founder. Choose one useful next action—watch a user try the flow, fix a point of confusion, or test a clearer message—and review what you learn.

## For You As A Young Man
Take the work seriously without turning every result into a judgment of your worth. Do the next honest, useful thing in front of you, then learn from what happened. You can want to win and still act well on a day when you do not.
