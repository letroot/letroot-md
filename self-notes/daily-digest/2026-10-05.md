# Morning Digest - 2026-10-05

## Main Thought
Resilience is built into the conditions of an ordinary week. Setbacks will come; sleep, food, movement, a supportive environment, and boundaries on distracting inputs make them easier to absorb. Treat these basics as operating infrastructure, not rewards you earn after work is done.

## For You As A Founder
Pick the weakest part of your personal operating setup and make one specific adjustment this week: set a consistent bedtime, prepare reliable meals, schedule movement, or limit news and social checks to fixed windows. The goal is not a perfect routine. It is a baseline that still holds when a launch, customer problem, or hard decision hits.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not build your identity around enduring preventable exhaustion. Discipline includes protecting the capacity you need to keep your word and make clear decisions over time. Set up your environment so the useful choice is easier on ordinary days.
