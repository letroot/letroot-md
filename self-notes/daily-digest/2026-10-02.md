# Morning Digest - 2026-10-02

## Main Thought
“Should we pivot?” is a diagnosis question, not a mood. Time on the idea, honest traction, founder alignment, and the quality of the next best option determine the answer. Weekly switching creates pivot hell; stubbornly grinding on a dead idea creates a different kind of blindness.

## For You As A Founder
Run a short doctor exam on your company: How long have you pursued this direction? What does the evidence actually say? Are the founders aligned about the situation? What are the top three alternatives, and which one has real conviction behind it? If you have no credible next option, talk to customers and learn before pivoting. If you are changing direction every week, freeze the decision for three months and define what would count as learning.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not confuse movement with courage. Sometimes courage means admitting that sunk work no longer matters; sometimes it means staying with one path long enough to learn what it can teach. Let evidence, conviction, and a defined time horizon decide, not social-media fashion or temporary frustration.
