# Morning Digest - 2026-10-20

## Main Thought
Cash flow is not an accounting detail to review after the important work; it is the time and choice that keep a young business alive. Felix Dennis's advice is unglamorous: watch money coming in and going out, keep fixed costs modest, and plan for the difficult case. A business can have a promising idea and still run out of time to make it work.

## For You As A Founder
Make runway visible. Review expected receipts, upcoming obligations, and the date cash could become tight. Follow up on overdue invoices, negotiate commitments before they become emergencies, and avoid locking the company into costs that assume best-case growth. This does not mean starving investment; it means knowing which spending buys customer value and which merely makes the business look successful.

## For You As A Young Man
Look directly at the numbers, even when they disappoint you. Optimism is useful for beginning, but it cannot substitute for a clear view of your obligations and limits. Prudence is not timidity: it gives you enough room to keep choosing, learning, and acting deliberately.
