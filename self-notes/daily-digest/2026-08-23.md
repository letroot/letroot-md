# Morning Digest - 2026-08-23

## Main Thought
The best tools become invisible: users spend their attention on the work, not on learning workarounds, defending defaults, or admiring the tool itself. Complexity is not a badge of seriousness. Good defaults are a product decision made once for many users; configuration is an escape hatch, not a substitute for judgment.

## For You As A Founder
Audit your product's common path. Where are users forced to decide things you should have decided for them, configure things that should work, or learn a quirk just to get basic value? Improve that path before adding another advanced option. Measure success by fewer mistakes, less time to outcome, and less need for explanation, not by how clever the system feels to its builders.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not confuse friction you have learned to tolerate with proof that the friction is valuable. A mature mind can separate identity from preference, notice what is actually wasting time, and change course without feeling diminished.
