# Morning Digest - 2026-11-05

## Main Thought
Taleb’s idea of *via negativa* is that, in complex systems, removing a harmful element can be safer than adding a clever fix. A new feature, rule, or hedge creates its own side effects; removing a brittle dependency or costly commitment can reduce risk at its source.

## For You As A Founder
When a business problem appears, do not default to adding another tool, process, or product layer. First ask what can be removed: a feature that creates support burden, a customer dependency that leaves you exposed, or a commitment that narrows your options. Subtraction is not automatically right, but it is a useful first test when interactions are hard to predict.

## For You As A Young Man
When something is not working, pause before piling on more effort or rules. Find the one avoidable source of trouble you can remove, then see whether the situation improves. Sound judgment often begins with making fewer things capable of going wrong.
