# Morning Digest - 2026-10-15

## Main Thought
Enterprise sales gets less mysterious when you learn how the customer already buys. If your pricing or offer forces a champion to invent a new approval process, you have added friction before they can even judge the value. Fit the buying motion they know, then make the outcome clear.

## For You As A Founder
Before pursuing a large account, ask someone who has bought similar software to walk you through the process: who evaluates it, which security or procurement checks apply, where budget comes from, and how pricing is approved. Shape one offer and price structure to fit that path. Learn the route before increasing sales activity.

## For You As A Young Man
Treat unfamiliar processes as skills you can learn. Ask direct questions, take careful notes, and prepare before making a big ask. Confidence grows from understanding how the other person makes a decision, not from pretending the process is simple.
