# Morning Digest - 2026-09-12

## Main Thought
Founder time is expensive because it is scarce, risky, and often paid for only after years of uncertainty. The useful question is not what your hour is worth on a timesheet; it is whether this task deserves the attention of the one person who must find product-market fit, talk to customers, set direction, and make the difficult tradeoffs.

## For You As A Founder
Make two lists. First: the work only you can do now, such as customer learning, sales, positioning, product judgment, and key hiring. Second: work that can be documented, automated, delayed, or done competently by someone else. Move one item off your plate this week, then protect the freed time for a task that could materially improve the company's odds rather than merely make you feel productive.

## For You As A Young Man
Respect your attention as a finite asset. Responsibility is not doing every task yourself; it is making sure the important things receive your best energy and the routine things have a reliable home.
