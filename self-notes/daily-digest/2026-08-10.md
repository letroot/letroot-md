# Morning Digest - 2026-08-10

## Main Thought
Creating value and capturing value are different jobs. A business can help customers, grow usage, and still be unable to earn durable returns if it remains easy to substitute or trapped in a market where every gain is competed away. The question is not only whether the product works. It is whether the company has a clear, defensible reason to exist and the power to be paid for it.

## For You As A Founder
Test your strategy with two questions: What specific value do we create for customers, and why can we retain a fair share of it over time? Look for real differentiation: a unique capability, trusted distribution, switching costs, proprietary insight, or a narrow market you can serve exceptionally well. Do not confuse busyness, revenue, or a crowded category with a durable business model.

## For You As A Young Man
Think independently before you imitate. The popular path may be popular because it is legible, not because it is right for you. Learn the principles beneath someone else's success, then apply them to the actual problem in front of you instead of repeating the visible surface of their playbook.
