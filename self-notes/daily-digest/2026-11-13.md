# Morning Digest - 2026-11-13

## Main Thought
A business can produce strong income and still depend almost entirely on its founder. Hormozi’s framework distinguishes expert-led businesses, where customers buy the founder’s personal skill, from a transferable asset that can reliably deliver value through a team and repeatable systems. Neither is automatically better; the important thing is to know which one you are building.

## For You As A Founder
Decide whether your goal is a profitable practice built around your expertise or a company that can operate without your constant presence. If you want transferability, identify where customer value currently depends on you and make delivery, relationships, and decision-making more repeatable. Don’t describe a founder-dependent service as a scalable asset before the evidence supports it.

## For You As A Young Man
Choose the kind of success you actually want before optimizing for someone else’s scoreboard. Personal mastery can be valuable; so can building something that works beyond you. Be honest about the tradeoffs, then make your daily choices match the destination.
