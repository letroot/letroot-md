# Morning Digest - 2026-10-16

## Main Thought
Jesse Cole's Savannah Bananas case shows what changes when a company measures the experience it promises. The team tracked concrete details such as how long fans waited for food or merchandise, then improved the operation around those moments. Revenue still matters, but it is a lagging scorecard; experience measures reveal sooner whether the product is delivering.

## For You As A Founder
Choose one moment in your customer journey that defines the value of your product. Measure how reliably and quickly customers reach it, then use the result to choose the next operational improvement. A metric tied to the promise can guide the team better than a growing dashboard of numbers that do not change a decision.

## For You As A Young Man
If you want to be dependable, measure the thing you actually promised to do. Specific standards make it easier to notice where people are waiting, confused, or let down, and to improve without relying on good intentions alone.
