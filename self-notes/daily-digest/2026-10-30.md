# Morning Digest - 2026-10-30

## Main Thought
AppLovin’s early consumer app was not its strongest asset; its recommendation engine was. Adam Foroughi and the team changed the company around the mechanism that was producing real value, turning that engine into an advertising platform. The case illustrates a useful distinction: the first form of a product can fail while the capability underneath it is still valuable.

## For You As A Founder
Look at what users return for, what performs reliably, and what creates measurable value. If that mechanism is stronger than the feature or business model wrapped around it, consider whether the product should be rebuilt around the mechanism. Do not preserve a weak wrapper just because it was your original idea.

## For You As A Young Man
Be willing to change your approach without treating that change as proof you were foolish to begin. Keep the goal of serving a real need; update the means when evidence shows a better path. Judgment is not stubbornness, and adaptability is not lack of conviction.
