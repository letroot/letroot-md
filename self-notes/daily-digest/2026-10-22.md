# Morning Digest - 2026-10-22

## Main Thought
Attention is only the beginning of distribution. In Grant Owen's marketing discussion, even a large awareness campaign can underperform if it leaves interested people nowhere useful to go. Attach a clear next step—such as a signup, demo, or useful resource—so attention can become a relationship and a measurable opportunity.

## For You As A Founder
For every campaign you run, define the action that follows the hook. Make that action easy to understand and easy to complete, then track whether people take it. Views can show that a message traveled; signups, qualified conversations, and purchases show whether your path turns attention into progress.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not confuse being noticed with making progress. In work and in life, decide what a good next step looks like, then make it clear enough that someone can take it. A little thought about the path after the first impression is more useful than chasing applause.
