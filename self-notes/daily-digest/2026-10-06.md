# Morning Digest - 2026-10-06

## Main Thought
Good sales begins with understanding the customer's problem, not performing a product monologue. If a prospect does not want the outcome, lowering the price will not create desire; it may only make them wonder what is wrong. Listen until you can explain their situation in their own terms, then show how your product helps.

## For You As A Founder
On your next sales call, spend the first part learning the customer's business: what must go right this week, what is currently getting in the way, and what happens if it stays unsolved? Only connect your product to the problem after you can describe it clearly. Record the customer's words, especially the moment they recognize relief or value, and use that language to improve your pitch.

## For You As A Young Man
Treat selling as a way to help someone make a sound decision. Be curious enough to hear a real "no," and honest enough not to push a deal that will not benefit the other person. Confidence can come from being useful, not from talking more.
