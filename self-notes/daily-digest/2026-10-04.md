# Morning Digest - 2026-10-04

## Main Thought
Success is not proof that a decision was wise, just as failure is not proof that it was foolish. In uncertain fields, luck can reward a weak process and punish a good one. Judge decisions by what was knowable when you made them, not by the story hindsight builds afterward.

## For You As A Founder
For an important bet, write down the evidence, assumptions, downside, and what would change your mind before results arrive. When you review it, compare the outcome with that record. Keep the parts of the process that were disciplined, revise the parts that relied on wishful thinking, and resist turning one win into a repeatable formula without evidence.

## For You As A Young Man
Work hard and prepare, but stay humble about what effort can control. Do not copy a successful person's visible habits and assume they caused the result; ask what role timing, selection, and chance played. Build habits that improve your odds without pretending they guarantee the outcome.
