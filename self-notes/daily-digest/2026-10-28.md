# Morning Digest - 2026-10-28

## Main Thought
Resilience is partly built before the hard week arrives. In their discussion of founder habits, Dalton Caldwell and Michael Seibel argue that sleep, food, exercise, and a supportive home environment are not perks after the work; they are preventative infrastructure that helps setbacks stay manageable. You do not need to prove toughness by running yourself down.

## For You As A Founder
Choose one basic support that is currently unreliable and make it easier to maintain: set a consistent bedtime, keep simple food available, schedule movement, or improve the place where you work and recover. Treat these as operating conditions for better decisions, not rewards you earn only after a launch.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not wait until you feel depleted to care for your body and surroundings. Small, ordinary habits protect your capacity to be patient, keep your word, and return to difficult work tomorrow. Discipline includes maintenance, not only pushing harder.
