# Morning Digest - 2026-11-01

## Main Thought
This week’s lessons are about staying in the game while learning what deserves to endure. End a partnership without letting ego turn it into a war; spend money only on a constraint you understand; look beneath headline numbers for hidden exposure; and build the health routines that make hard weeks survivable. In sales, let qualified customers’ responses inform price. In product, keep the value-creating engine even if the original wrapper needs to change.

## For You As A Founder
Before a major decision, ask two questions: what must I protect so I can keep operating, and what assumption can I test before committing further? Protect cash, trust, and your capacity to make good decisions. Then run a contained test or inspect real usage. Adapt the tactic when evidence changes, without abandoning the underlying customer value that is working.

## For You As A Young Man
Durable progress is neither stubborn endurance nor constant reinvention. Be steady about your principles and flexible about your methods. Take care of the foundations, look honestly at the evidence, and choose the next step without needing to win every argument or prove every first idea right.
