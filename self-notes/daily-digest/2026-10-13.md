# Morning Digest - 2026-10-13

## Main Thought
A disciplined pursuit outside work can strengthen your ability to stay with difficult things. Choose something meaningful and concrete, schedule it, and keep returning even when progress stalls. At the plateau, curiosity and showing up matter more than tracking a perfect streak or chasing endless optimization.

## For You As A Founder
Pick a practice that is not tied to company performance—lifting, writing, music, or another craft—and size it to your actual week. Give it a repeatable time and a simple measure of showing up. It gives your attention a place to train and keeps every sense of progress from depending on startup results.

## For You As A Young Man
Do hard things quietly and consistently. Let the practice teach patience and humility; when improvement slows, explore the work more deeply instead of abandoning it for a new hack or turning discipline into a performance for other people.
