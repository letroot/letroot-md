# Morning Digest - 2026-11-14

## Main Thought
A customer’s requested feature may be only one step toward a bigger goal. Jason Cohen’s Needs Stack asks founders to follow that chain upward: the customer may want a tool to complete a task, but ultimately want a business result or personal outcome. A company one level higher can sometimes make the lower-level product unnecessary by helping customers reach that end more directly.

## For You As A Founder
When a customer asks for a feature, write down the immediate job and then ask what success that job enables. Look for competitors beyond products with similar screens: a service, internal hire, workaround, or different workflow may solve the higher-level need. This can reveal a stronger product direction or show where your current offer needs to connect to the customer’s real goal.

## For You As A Young Man
Listen for what people are trying to accomplish, not just the words they use to ask for help. Understanding the purpose behind a request lets you offer something more useful than a literal response.
