# Morning Digest - 2026-10-19

## Main Thought
Explain your product without naming its technology. In Kelsey Hightower's startup test, removing the word “AI” forces a team to say what it actually does for someone. If the value disappears with the buzzword, the product story may be covering for an unclear problem or outcome.

## For You As A Founder
Rewrite your pitch in plain language: who has what problem, and what changes after using your product? Then check whether customers recognize that outcome in their own words. Technology can be an advantage, but it is not the reason a customer buys unless it produces a concrete result they care about.

## For You As A Young Man
Practice clarity before confidence. It is tempting to lean on impressive labels when the underlying idea is still fuzzy. Strip the jargon away, state what you know, and admit what you still need to learn. That habit makes your judgment more trustworthy.
