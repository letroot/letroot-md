# Morning Digest - 2026-10-07

## Main Thought
Christina Cacioppo tried many ideas before finding the problem that became Vanta. Her "bad art" frame makes early attempts less precious: rough ideas are part of the search, and each one can teach you where real demand lives. The aim is not to defend every attempt; it is to learn cheaply enough to keep looking.

## For You As A Founder
Before spending weeks building, test the riskiest assumption with a customer. Try selling the outcome or delivering it manually first. Treat polite interest as weak evidence; look for someone who wants it now, commits time, or pays. If the signal is absent, take the learning and move on without turning the experiment into a verdict on you.

## For You As A Young Man
Separate your worth from the first draft of your work. Make the attempt, let reality respond, and use what happened to choose the next one. Humility is being willing to revise; persistence is continuing the search, not clinging to one idea.
