# Morning Digest - 2026-10-09

## Main Thought
In Tommy Mello's account of building A1 Garage Door Service, one useful operating principle is to stop assigning important work by founder preference alone. Notice which necessary tasks you consistently avoid or do poorly, then create capacity around that gap. A company becomes stronger when its structure covers the founder's weaknesses instead of hiding them.

## For You As A Founder
List the recurring jobs your business needs, and mark the ones you avoid, delay, or perform below the standard the role requires. Choose one costly gap and address it deliberately: document the process, automate part of it, or find a person whose strengths complement yours. Keep ownership of the result even when you delegate the task.

## For You As A Young Man
Self-awareness is useful only when it changes how you act. Admit where your ability or interest is limited, ask for help without embarrassment, and respect the people whose strengths fill the gaps. You do not need to become good at everything to become dependable.
