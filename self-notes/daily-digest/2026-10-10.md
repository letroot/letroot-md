# Morning Digest - 2026-10-10

## Main Thought
Some business opportunities stay profitable because competitors never look for them. They may be hidden in fragmented data, local workflows, or work dismissed as boring or low-status. The overlooked quality is not automatically a moat, but it can give a thoughtful operator room to learn and build before the crowd arrives.

## For You As A Founder
Pick one essential industry you usually ignore and map a specific workflow around it: who does the work, what breaks, what it costs, and what people already do to cope. Talk with someone who runs that process before proposing software. Look for a narrow, repeatable pain that customers value enough to change, rather than assuming that a glamorous market is the only place worth building.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not let status decide what deserves your attention. Being willing to study ordinary work patiently can reveal problems that trend-chasing misses. Respect the people who do that work, and earn your conclusions through direct contact with it.
