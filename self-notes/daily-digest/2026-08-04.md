# Morning Digest - 2026-08-04

## Main Thought
A bad ten minutes can produce a decision with a long shadow. Anger, fear, humiliation, and urgency are real signals, but they narrow the field of view and tempt you toward actions that are hard to reverse. Strength is not pretending not to feel them. It is creating enough space to decide what the emotion should fuel and what it should never be allowed to decide.

## For You As A Founder
Make a rule for high-emotion moments: do not send the message, fire the person, cut the price, kill the project, or make the public declaration until you have named the trigger and slept on it when possible. Then use the energy productively: investigate the failure, write the hard brief, make the next sales call, or fix the system that allowed the problem. Pause the irreversible act; accelerate the useful one.

## For You As A Young Man
Self-command is most visible when you are provoked. Take the walk, leave the room, train, write the uncensored draft and do not send it. You are not weak for feeling heat; you become dependable by refusing to hand the steering wheel to it.
