# Morning Digest - 2026-10-29

## Main Thought
Your close rate can tell you whether the price is doing its job. Alex Hormozi frames repeated, easy yeses as a possible sign that the offer is priced below the value customers see. But a low close rate does not automatically mean “lower the price”; it can signal weak qualification or an offer that does not solve a compelling problem.

## For You As A Founder
Track close rate alongside who you spoke with and what they believed they would get. If well-qualified prospects accept with little hesitation and delivery capacity is tight, test a higher price with the next cohort. If prospects hesitate, learn why before changing the number. Treat pricing as an experiment, not a referendum on your worth.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not set terms only to avoid the discomfort of hearing no. Make a fair proposal, explain the value honestly, and let the other person decide. A respectful rejection is information; an easy yes can be information too.
