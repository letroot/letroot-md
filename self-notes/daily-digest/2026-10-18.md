# Morning Digest - 2026-10-18

## Main Thought
Choose the game your ambition actually fits. Venture capital expects a path to an unusually large outcome; a profitable small business can be an excellent choice, but it should not carry venture-scale costs and expectations without a credible route to scale. Ambition is most useful when paired with a clear chain of plausible steps.

## For You As A Founder
Write down what success could look like for your business in ten years. Then ask what kind of company, funding, growth rate, and tradeoffs that outcome requires. If the goal is modest, choose a model built to be profitable and durable. If you want a much larger outcome, identify the key leaps in the plan and test the riskiest one with evidence.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not shrink your aim just to avoid being judged, and do not mistake a grand claim for a plan. Name what you want, explain how each step could work, and let evidence sharpen the path. Choose ambition you are willing to support with patient work.
