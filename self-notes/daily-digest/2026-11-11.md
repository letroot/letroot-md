# Morning Digest - 2026-11-11

## Main Thought
Vic Keller’s hiring framework is the three C’s: character, competence, and chemistry. Skill matters, but a person who cannot be trusted or who steadily damages how a team works can cost more than their output adds. The best hire is not just impressive; they make the whole group more capable.

## For You As A Founder
Before bringing someone into a high-trust role, define what integrity, ability to learn, and productive collaboration look like in observable behavior. Check all three with examples and references rather than letting charisma or a polished résumé stand in for evidence. The same framework applies to contractors and co-founders.

## For You As A Young Man
Build the qualities you would want in a teammate: keep commitments, stay curious when you are wrong, and make other people’s work better. Talent opens doors, but reliability and good partnership are what make people want to work with you again.
