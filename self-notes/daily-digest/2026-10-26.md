# Morning Digest - 2026-10-26

## Main Thought
Money can accelerate work once you know what needs doing; it cannot buy the judgment that identifies the right work. Jason Cohen's startup exercise separates execution constraints from exploration: funding may let you ship more code or run more campaigns, but it cannot tell you what customers value or why they hesitate. Capital magnifies the operating system you already have, whether clear or confused.

## For You As A Founder
Before taking on a new cost, name the exact bottleneck it removes and the evidence that the bottleneck is real. If the missing piece is customer understanding, a sharper product decision, or a message that resonates, buy learning first through conversations and small tests. Spend to scale a working path, not to avoid discovering whether the path works.

## For You As A Young Man
Do not wait for better tools or more resources to develop judgment. Use what you have to get closer to the truth, make a decision, and learn from its result. Resourcefulness is not doing everything cheaply; it is knowing what money can solve and what work remains yours.
