# Fellowship and the Reality of the Heavenly World

The reality of the heavenly world is not one the believer can ignore. It is at the core of our faith and the reality of God's purpose. In Ephesians 1, Paul tells us that God's purpose is sonship. We have studied Romans 8:15–17: sonship is to be God's child as well as his heir. We are called to fellowship with him, to walk in his nature and character, and to carry out the assignment he has allotted to us.

Ephesians 2:18 shows us how we fellowship with God: “Through him”—the Lord Jesus—“we both have access by one Spirit to the Father.” Via the work of redemption Christ has done, he has brought us back to God's desire and intention from the start: to be with him, to fellowship with him. Through him we have access by one Spirit to the Father.

We know who we have access to. But there is a question to answer: where do we have access to? Where is the Father for us to have access to him?
